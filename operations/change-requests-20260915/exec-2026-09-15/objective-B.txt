Voice Mode holds multiple worker lanes in ONE tab: a lane strip makes every lane identifiable and shows which is audible and which has the operator's floor; the operator can switch the lane their voice is addressed to, live, without leaving the screen; a fourth lane asks instead of appearing; one floor-scheduling rule prevents any lane from speaking over the operator's capture; and talkerBus results are correlated to their request and lane so a stale or foreign result can never populate the active lane's card. TDD tests cover the lane behaviour and the correlation rules, a browser-level harness covers the multi-lane case, and single-lane behaviour is provably unchanged. Invariants: capture is never gated by the scheduler, the operator's floor is never interrupted, no merge, no production restart.

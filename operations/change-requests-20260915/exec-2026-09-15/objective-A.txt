The weekly Command Code catalogue refresh script restarts pi-web-ui safely and identifiably: it decides idleness from the live busy-session count (never the active-turn counter), it restarts only through scripts/restart-pi-web-ui.sh with an explicit reason, and when the service is busy, or the busy count cannot be determined, it does not restart at all. TDD tests cover the busy, idle, unreachable-API and unreachable-capacity cases, and the test suite, typecheck and lint all pass in the worktree. Invariants: no merge, no production restart, no path outside the owned list is touched.

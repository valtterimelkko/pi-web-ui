## Findings for R5

1. **Wave K entry evidence: a silent stall plus a monitoring gap** (E2a-6c, E2a-6).
   - **The stall:** after a hard kill or a drain timeout, goal children are not paused. They sit `running` while idle, and nothing reaches the parent. In practice the parent had to send 8 follow-ups, and each child lost about 10 idle minutes.
   - **Wave K's design answers the continuation gap:** K2, "continue once", would fix it.
   - **What K2 does not cover:** the interruption is invisible to parents' watches, an operator-surface gap.
   - **Transient goal ends are rare on the target route:** 1 of 23. So K's value is restart recovery more than provider transients.
   - **Second goal-loop symptom:** a reviewer blocked waiting for its parent was re-prompted about 20 times by its own goal loop until the no-progress guard paused it.
2. **Admission-accounting leak** (production, a defect candidate).
   - **What happened:** `/capacity` counted 8, then 10, quarantined turns with no non-terminal receipts behind them, starting at 04:08 UTC around parent steers into goal sessions.
   - **Effect:** production refused new work for `memory_pressure` while the host had about 19 GiB free. Only a restart cleared it.
   - **Status:** reproduce it before any fix. Evidence: `/root/orch-ops/orchestration-scaling/e2/debt-evidence-20261003T074450Z/`.
3. **The admission ceiling:** about 9 concurrent turns in production (E2a-4).
   - **Mechanism (hypothesis):** 512 MiB reserved per turn, against the service's 8 GiB limit and a 1.5 GiB floor, at `server/src/internal-api/admission-controller.ts:730-732` and `server/src/config.ts:740-747`.
   - **Effect:** `maxActiveTurns=16` is memory-bound to about half its nominal value.
   - **Decision for R5:** a tuning decision, then a coordinated re-measure of E2a-2.
4. **Restart path:** the Agent OS pre-tool gate does not match `npm run production:drain-restart`. The 08:14 restart needed no `--force`, but the gate's intent and the canonical command disagree. Owner decision.
5. **Process lessons:**
   - Long live arms must run as supervised units with cleanup outside the tool call: a foreground command was cut off around 15 minutes and skipped its trap.
   - Children must never run housekeeping or npm in shared trees (two incidents, below).
   - Evidence must be copied before anything is deleted (two losses).
